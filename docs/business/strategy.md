# Business strategy

StackWorkbench serves backend, enterprise integration, application support, QA, DevOps, and data integration engineers who diagnose issues across systems. The initial product is Log Inspector, focused on a repeatable job engineers can complete in the browser.

Free manual tools can earn discovery and trust by solving a task quickly and keeping sensitive inputs local. Paid workflows can justify recurring spend through recurring value, such as scheduled checks, repeatable analysis, or team collaboration. Prioritize products based on evidence of repeated use and willingness to pay.

The initial commercial objective is **$50 in monthly recurring revenue**. A small team should favor tools it can operate sustainably, with predictable infrastructure costs and support demands. Validate the problem, workflow, and willingness to pay before building hosted integrations for it.
